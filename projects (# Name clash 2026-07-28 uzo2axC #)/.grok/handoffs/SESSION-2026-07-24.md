- **When:** after B1 stable; candidate for “next exp” vs B2 content flood.

- **Idea:** “At your current rate → B1 in ~X weeks, B2 in ~Y weeks.”

- **Feasible** with rolling averages (no ML); avoid false precision; min-data gate.
