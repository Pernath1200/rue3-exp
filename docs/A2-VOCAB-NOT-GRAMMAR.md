# A2 on RUE3 = vocab thickening, not grammar

**Locked (exp redesign 2026-07-20).**  
**App split:** RUE2 = **roots** (grammar). RUE3 = **trunk + leaves** (lexis).

---

## One sentence

**RUE3 A2 thickens high-frequency words and phrases (mostly by re-practising A1) and grows a fuller topic canopy. Tense/aspect/comparison as systems stay in RUE2.**

---

## What A2 is for

| Job | How |
|-----|-----|
| **Thicken trunk** | Recycle A1 frames with denser slots · high-freq A2 **words** in simple frames · lexical **chunks** |
| **Grow canopy** | Theme leaves (Home, Work, Travel, Food…) with Oxford A2 domain lexis |
| **Automate** | SRS / Today reviews — fruit units return without a second grammar tree |

## What A2 is not

| Out | Why |
|-----|-----|
| Past / perfect / future **units** | RUE2 verb-phrase root |
| Comparatives **as structure** | RUE2 NP / adj grammar |
| Quantity **as structure** (some/any drills as grammar) | RUE2 |
| Time preps **as structure** (in/on/at lessons) | RUE2 |
| A tall “grammar shaft” on the tree | Confuses products |

Parked wrong packs (if present): `data/blocks/_parked_grammar/`.

---

## A2 trunk (max 3 nodes)

1. **Core · recycle** — A1-style frames, denser lexis, gap on **content word**  
2. **Core · A2 lexis** — high-freq A2 verbs/adj/adv in **present** models, gap on **lemma**  
3. **Everyday chunks** — phrases (`I'd like…`, `all right`, `according to…`) · `V_PHR`

## A2 canopy

Topic leaves tagged to Codex `V_THM-A1B1-*` / COR for abstract words. Sentence mode = fruit.

Mega dumps (Describing / Verbs / Adverbs leaves) are **temporary experiment bulk** — reshape into themes later; they are still **lexis**, not grammar floors.
